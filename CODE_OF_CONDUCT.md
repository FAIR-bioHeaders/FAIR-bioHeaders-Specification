# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels; give constructive feedback on the work, acknowledge mistakes,
and avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure
of another person's private information. These expectations apply to issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project activities.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, warn participants, limit access,
or remove repeat offenders. Handle reports confidentially where possible,
consider the affected person's needs, document decisions privately, and recuse
maintainers who are involved in a complaint. A reviewer uninvolved in the original
decision should handle appeals.

## Private reporting and appeals

Report conduct concerns privately to [molik@fhref.org](mailto:molik@fhref.org). Include relevant links, what happened, and any immediate safety or confidentiality concerns. Do not post personal allegations or sensitive information in public issues.

Reports involving David Molik should go directly to [Adam Wright](mailto:wright@fhref.org) at [wright@fhref.org](mailto:wright@fhref.org). Reports involving Adam Wright should go directly to David at [molik@fhref.org](mailto:molik@fhref.org). Do not copy the person involved in the complaint.

Request an appeal from the contact who was not involved in the original decision. Anyone involved in a complaint or decision must recuse themselves from reviewing that report or appeal. If both maintainers are involved, an independent reviewer must be appointed; neither maintainer should decide the complaint or appeal.

### Proposal under review (issue #36)

The proposal under review for issue #36 fills the gap over who appoints that reviewer. It is not adopted and has no effect until the maintainers adopt it; until then, the addresses and routing above remain in effect.

Under the proposal, the independent reviewer is appointed under the procedure and deadlines in GOVERNANCE.md, using a private call for a conduct matter: a call for consenting candidates with no involvement or conflict, selection from that call under the conflict and objection rules there, one peremptory objection for each maintainer who is the subject or a party, and appointment and decision deadlines with host escalation. The reporter is told the proposed candidate and may object before the seven-day objection period ends. If the reporter objects, that candidate is not appointed; the next consenting candidate is proposed and the same objection period applies, within the same twenty-one-day appointment deadline. If no reviewer is appointed by the appointment deadline, or the reviewer does not decide by the decision deadline, the report stays open, the failure is recorded, and the reporter may escalate through the repository host's abuse-reporting process. The appointed reviewer, not the maintainers, decides the complaint or appeal.

Where a maintainer is the subject of the report, a party to the matter, or the subject of a removal case under GOVERNANCE.md, that maintainer's agreement is not required; each maintainer who is the subject or a party may object once, without giving reasons; and the appointed reviewer serves for both the report and the removal case. A maintainer who is the subject or a party may not select the candidate or control the appointment.

### Vacancy routing under the proposal

If a report would go to a named maintainer under the paragraphs above but that maintainer no longer serves, it goes to the remaining maintainer if that person is not involved. If no uninvolved maintainer remains, the reporter may use the repository host's abuse-reporting process and ask the host to route the report to an independent reviewer appointed under GOVERNANCE.md. The proposal does not create a new private address. A report involving the only remaining maintainer follows the same route.

The related proposal for independent appeals, reviewer appointment, vacancies, removal and disputed conflicts is drafted in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). It remains unadopted and creates no reviewer or new contact route until the maintainers record its adoption.
