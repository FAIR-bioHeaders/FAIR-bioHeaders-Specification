# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels; give constructive feedback on the work, acknowledge mistakes,
and avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, and disclosure
of another person's private information. These expectations apply to issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project activities.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, warn participants, limit access,
or remove repeat offenders. Handle reports confidentially where possible,
consider the affected person's needs, document decisions privately, and recuse
maintainers who are involved in a complaint. A reviewer uninvolved in the original
decision should handle appeals.

## Private reporting and appeals

Report conduct concerns privately to [molik@fhref.org](mailto:molik@fhref.org). Include relevant links, what happened, and any immediate safety or confidentiality concerns. Do not post personal allegations or sensitive information in public issues.

Reports involving David Molik should go directly to [Adam Wright](mailto:wright@fhref.org) at [wright@fhref.org](mailto:wright@fhref.org). Reports involving Adam Wright should go directly to David at [molik@fhref.org](mailto:molik@fhref.org). Do not copy the person involved in the complaint.

Request an appeal from the contact who was not involved in the original decision. Anyone involved in a complaint or decision must recuse themselves from reviewing that report or appeal. If both maintainers are involved, an independent reviewer must be appointed; neither maintainer should decide the complaint or appeal.

The proposal under review for issue #36 fills the gap over who appoints that reviewer. It is not adopted and has no effect until the maintainers adopt it. Under it, the independent reviewer is appointed by agreement between the two maintainers and the person who reported the concern, choosing someone with no involvement in the report and no conflict of interest; neither maintainer appoints a reviewer alone. The reporter is told who was appointed and may object before the review begins. If the maintainers and the reporter cannot agree on a reviewer, the report stays open, the maintainers record that no independent reviewer could be appointed, and the reporter is told that they may escalate through the repository host's own abuse-reporting process. The appointed reviewer, not the maintainers, decides the complaint or appeal. Where a maintainer is the subject of the report, a party to the matter, or the subject of a removal case under GOVERNANCE.md, that maintainer's agreement is not required: the reviewer is proposed by the other maintainer or, if the other maintainer is also a subject or party, by the reporter; each affected maintainer may object once, without giving reasons; and the appointed reviewer serves for both the report and the removal case. If no reviewer can be appointed in that situation, the report stays open and the reporter may escalate through the repository host's abuse-reporting process. The related proposal for independent appeals, vacancies, removal and disputed conflicts is drafted in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
